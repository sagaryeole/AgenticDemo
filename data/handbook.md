# Riverside Community Library Handbook

## Opening hours
The library is open Tuesday to Friday from 9:00 to 18:00 and on Saturday from 10:00 to 16:00. We are closed on Sunday and Monday.

On public holidays the library is closed. The holiday schedule is posted on the front door two weeks in advance.

## Membership
Membership is free for residents of Riverside. Visitors from other towns pay a yearly fee of 15 dollars. To join, bring a photo ID and a proof of address, such as a utility bill.

Children under 14 need a parent or guardian to sign the application form. Each member receives a library card with a seven-digit number. If you lose your card, a replacement costs 3 dollars.

## Borrowing rules
Members can borrow up to 8 books at the same time. Each book can be kept for 21 days. DVDs and audiobooks can be kept for 7 days, and you may borrow at most 2 of them at once.

Reference books and the newspaper archive cannot leave the building. You can renew an item twice, as long as nobody else has reserved it. Renewals can be made online, at the front desk, or by calling 555-0142.

## Late fees and lost items
Late books cost 25 cents per day, up to a maximum of 5 dollars per book. DVDs cost 1 dollar per day, up to 10 dollars. Children's books are never fined.

If an item is more than 60 days late, it is considered lost and you will be charged the replacement cost plus a 5 dollar handling fee. Borrowing is blocked while your unpaid fees are higher than 10 dollars. Fees can be paid at the desk, by card or in cash.

## Study rooms
The library has four study rooms, each for up to six people. Rooms can be booked for up to 2 hours per day, up to 7 days in advance, using the online booking page or at the front desk.

A booking is cancelled automatically if nobody arrives within 15 minutes of the start time. Food is not allowed in the study rooms, but water in a closed bottle is fine.

## Computers and printing
There are 12 public computers on the ground floor. Each session lasts 60 minutes and can be extended once if no one is waiting.

Printing costs 10 cents per black-and-white page and 50 cents per colour page. Wi-Fi is free: connect to the network "Riverside-Guest". No password is needed.

## Events and children's programs
Story time for children aged 3 to 6 takes place every Wednesday at 10:30 and every first Saturday of the month at 11:00. The Coding Club for ages 10 to 14 meets on Thursdays at 16:00 and needs registration through form LB-204.

The book club for adults meets on the last Friday of every month at 18:30. All events are free.

## Accessibility
The building has a step-free entrance on the east side and an elevator to the first floor. Large-print books and audiobooks are available on the ground floor. Assistance dogs are welcome.

Staff can bring books to the reading area for visitors who have difficulty walking; just ask at the front desk. A home delivery service is available for members over 75 or with a disability. It needs form LB-310.

## Donations
The library accepts donated books in good condition on Tuesdays and Thursdays between 10:00 and 12:00. We cannot accept encyclopedias older than 2010, textbooks, or damaged books. Donors can ask for a receipt.
